## Method and caveats

- Series and tier come from `outputs/series_clean.csv`; map/round/player detail from
  `data/raw/cs2_all_tiers_games.csv` (`is_total` rows dropped — they are series summaries).
- Map coverage is incomplete in the source: the window's series played more maps than the raw
  table has rows for, and a few series have none. Map-level numbers are labelled with their
  coverage in the Events table and are lower bounds wherever it is below 100%.
- Series results come from the `winner` column, which agrees with the series scores on all 64
  series. (An earlier cut of this script XOR-ed that team-NAME field with the side flag and
  silently inverted every series Vitality played as team2 — the reason the record was re-derived
  from the score columns before anything was reported.)
- Elo is the repo's walk-forward engine (K=32, base 1500) replayed over the full 9,920-series
  history, so a rating entering the window reflects everything before it. Pre-match ratings only.
- The window is the pair of dates requested. The data's own edges inside it are shown in the
  Headline table; the last Vitality series in the source data lands before 2026-05-05.
- Opponent Elo is the *pre-match* rating of Vitality's opponent in each series.
